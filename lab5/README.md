# Tampa Bay Vessel Flow Map by Sydney Vanessa Campbell

## Study Area and Dates:
For my assignment I decided to focus on Tampa Bay in Florida. I used AcccessAIS as my data source to study the vessel activity there from January 1, 2023 to January 8, 2023. Because AccessAIS uses UTC timestamps and Tampa Bay uses Eastern Standard Time, my data actually begins on December 31, 2022 and ends on January 8, 2023. I decided to choose Tampa Bay because I wanted to learn more about the vessel activity in the area. I knew that its a highly populated area that is also near an Air Force Base (MCF - Macdill Air Force Base), so I thought my map would show a lot of activity between each vessel type.I chose the time range because I wanted to specifically gain insight on how vessel activity flowed within one week of time.   

## Interpretation:
I noticed that the 'Other Vessels' vessel type experienced a lot more traction throughout the time frame in comparison to the other vessel types. This wasn't much of a surprise because Tampa Bay, and Florida as a whole, is a very recreational area. Because of the weather and proximity to water and the ocean, its unsurprising that a lot of the vessel activity is primarily recreational (Other vessels: fishing, sailing, pleasure craft, tugs, and more (Lab 5 Millions of Data, Flow Map)). I also noticed a pattern of the Tanker vessels going to and from the same area on the map, near the airport but not in the exact same places.
